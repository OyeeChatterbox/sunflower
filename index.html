A quiet thought

Just between us.

Sabse zyada maine ek cheez sochi hai... ki shayad main tumhe pasand karta hoon. Actually, pasand toh karta hi hoon, but kabhi-kabhi mujhe genuinely lagta hai ki shayad main tumhare liye theek insaan nahi hoon.

Ye thought pata nahi kyun aata hai, but jab bhi tumhe observe karta hoon na, tab ye cheez aur deep lagne lagti hai.

The way you look at me sometimes—apni aankhein thodi wide karke, jaise sach mein mera pura dimaag ek baar me read karne ki koshish kar rahi ho. Aur baat karte-karte wo ajeeb sa, funny tareeka jisme tum apna sar hilati ho. Wo bina kisi reason ke playfully idhar-udhar jump karna, chalte-chalte achanak playful ho jana...

Aur sabse achhi baat: tum kisi ko impress karne ke liye apna koi fake version nahi banati. You don't change who you are just because other people expect it. Situations chahe jaisi bhi hon, tum adapt kar leti ho without completely changing yourself.

And honestly, jab main ye sab dekhta hoon na, toh mujhe lagta hai ki tum kitni genuine, kitni special insaan ho. Aur shayad isi wajah se mere dimaag mein ek simple sa sawaal aa jata hai:

“Kya main actually tumhare liye sahi hoon?”

Aisa nahi hai ki tum literally perfect ho, aur na hi main koi dramatic baatein bolna chahta hoon. But meri aankhon mein dekhogi toh tum mujhe mujhse kaafi behtar lagti ho. Tumhe dekh ke kabhi-kabhi lagta hai ki tumhari life mein shayad koi bohot achha insaan likha hoga. Koi jo tumhare liye mujhse better suited ho... koi jo tumhe wo sab de sake jo tum actually deserve karti ho. Aur mujhe sach mein nahi pata ki wo insaan main hoon ya nahi.

Ye main isliye nahi bol raha ki tum mere liye bura feel karo, ya ye koi self-pity hai. Aisa bilkul nahi hai. It's simply an honest thought jo mere andar kaafi time se thi.

Bas... ab mujhe nahi pata aur kya bolun.
Honestly, mere paas ab words nahi hain.
Jo tha, woh shayad bol diya.
Bas tumhe batana tha.
